# Changelog

## 0.3.0 — 2026-08-08

- **Freed the chapter's structure.** The ten-section arc is now a **coverage map and a
  proven default order — not a mandatory skeleton**. The outline is designed per topic:
  domains may be reordered, split, merged, renamed, or nested; H3/H4 sub-headings are
  legal anywhere (the old H3-only-inside-Treatment rule is gone); a long section is
  subdivided, not amputated ("cut, don't chunk" is gone). What survives every reshaping:
  each coverage domain is present somewhere or consciously excluded and named in the
  preface, and treatment never absorbs the review — coverage is the contract, structure
  is not.
- **Made depth contractual.** New depth contract, born from real use (reviews were
  over-summarizing): load-bearing studies reported as developed paragraphs (design,
  population, n, comparator, endpoint, effect size with CI), mechanism and background
  woven in, and compression named as a failure condition.
- **Removed the 15–30 source ceiling.** Sources are capped by relevance, never by count —
  past reviews had already been forced to exceed the budget repeatedly; the budget lost.
- **References + evals updated to match**: `review-arc.md` reframed as the coverage map
  (structuring guidance replaces merge rules; "Depth, not budget" replaces the budget);
  evals updated and extended with adaptive-structure and depth-over-compression cases.
- **Added README.md and this changelog** (refine-plugin audit gap).
