# Gate A2 — turnaround views for Meshy

Inputs: `idea.md`, `images/chosen-concept.png` (the concept the user picked), `images/front.png`, `images/side.png`, `images/back.png`.

These three images go to `meshy_multi_image_to_3d` next, which costs 15–30 credits per try.
Judge them as Meshy input: anything Meshy would misread becomes a bad mesh.

## Criteria

Give one score (1–5) for each:

- `one-view-each` — each image holds exactly one view (no grids, no extra figures, no text).
- `consistency` — front matches the chosen concept, and side and back match the front: proportions, palette, costume, props, height in frame.
- `orthographic` — front is straight on, side is a 90° profile, back is 180°; low perspective distortion.
- `meshy-readiness` — full body centred with margin, plain light grey background, soft even light, no cast shadows; humanoids in the same A- or T-pose in all three.

## Severity

- `blocker` — an image is unusable (wrong view, multi-panel, a different character).
- `major` — a mismatch Meshy will bake in (a prop missing in one view, pose differs, cropped limb, heavy shadow).
- `minor` — small colour or lighting drift, tight margins.

## Verdict and scores

- `PASS` when no `blocker` or `major` issue remains; minor issues may still be listed. Otherwise `REVISE`.
- Score each criterion: 5 no issue · 4 minor issues only · 3 one major · 2 several majors · 1 a blocker.
- Use the criterion ids above verbatim in `scores` and `issues[].criterion`, one score per criterion.
- One issue per defect; `id` is `<gate>-<n>` (`A1-1`, `C-3`); `credits` is the price from the list below, 0 for a free fix.
- A fix the user must decide (a different build, a different pick) has credits 0 and its `fix` starts with "user choice:".

## Fixes and their credits

- Crop, pad, recentre, or flatten the background with Pillow: 0. A cast shadow on the figure cannot be removed this way: it needs a regenerated view (9).
- Regenerate one view with `meshy_image_to_image` (`nano-banana-pro`, `reference_file_paths` = `chosen-concept.png`, plus `front.png` when regenerating side or back): 9 per image. Say which view and what must change.

Write `evidence` as "<view>: <what you see>".
