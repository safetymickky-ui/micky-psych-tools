## PR-01 [high] No claim-level verification pass before the report is shown and filed; verdict, adjudication and summary sentences misstate their sources

EVIDENCE: The only fidelity rule is plugins/pubmed-research-note/skills/pubmed-research-note/SKILL.md:228 'Anything you could not source is marked `[unverified]` in place', which covers claims with no source, not claims that misstate one. Measured, three independent reports: (1) run-A/fidelity.md: 133 claims, 7 misquoted + 4 unsupported; 7 of the 11 sit in the Verdict or adjudication (20.6% vs 4.0% elsewhere); per-study paragraphs 48/48 accurate. Examples: report.md:8 'the one diary-verified placebo-controlled trial' vs the full text (PMC6103648) 'We were not able to objectively verify menstrual status and had to rely on self-report'; report.md:12 'about half of women on risperidone normalise prolactin in double-blind trials (45-47%)' - the 45% is women on mixed prolactin-raising drugs and the 46.7% is a 15-man/15-woman sample whose women stayed 'well above the normal range' (PMC4998932); report.md:18 'every placebo-controlled trial required contraception' (only one documents it); report.md:101 'a naturalistic cohort (n=177)' is an amisulpride-induced subgroup, unstated. (2) audit-vault/supplements.md + oral.md: 9 of 59 sampled claims misquoted, 4 unsupported: 'Cognition was never a primary endpoint' (COSMOS-Mind NCT03035201's registered primary is global cognition); pooling 'three looks at the same cohort' (source pooled non-overlapping participants); vitamin D effect 'confined to deficiency' (source: 'stronger'); 'still a preprint' (PMID 38234846 has UpdateIn Schizophr Res 2024;270:392-402); stress findings 'real, clinically actionable' (source rates certainty limited/very low, n=170). (3) run-B/fidelity.md: 0 numeric misquotes but 3 unsupported synthesis sentences (C77, C81, C85): the heading at report.md:146 'แม้ IV data ก็อ่อนใน unipolar MDD' is contradicted by :152 (MDD-only RCT, d=0.75, NNT 4) and :153 (MA SMD -0.69); :133 says Cochrane 'ยืนยันว่ามีผลต่อ depression ไม่ใช่ SI' (the review has no SI outcome); :143 calls SSI/MADRS-10 'sensitive กว่า', which the cited pooled paper contradicts.

IMPACT: Errors enter when the agent summarises and generalises, and they cluster exactly where they carry the verdict: the run-A confidence clause rests on a false premise ('diary-verified' vs self-report); the patient-facing 'about half of women on risperidone' is not what the sources measured and would be quoted to the patient; misstatements sit among the multivitamin 'four things that kill it'; residents would be taught that IV-ketamine evidence is weak in unipolar MDD, the opposite of the report's own studies. v1.7.0's depth contract puts more numbers in each report without a verification step, widening the surface.

Keep a claim ledger outside the report covering every number and every characterisation of a source in the Verdict, adjudication, headings and summary sentences: design, blinding/'diary-verified'/ITT, population, which subgroup the n belongs to, primary vs secondary endpoint, the source's own certainty rating, publication status. Re-open the source passage (including methods/limitations) for each; a generalisation ('women on drug X') must match the population that produced the number; re-read each heading and summary sentence against the numbers beneath it. Fix a mismatch or mark it [unverified] in its own sentence. Add drift tells: 'a study's quality attribute not taken from its own methods or limitations' and 'a heading contradicted by the numbers under it'. Add eval cases with mocked sources that plant traps (subgroup n vs total n, an author certainty rating, an UpdateIn link, a self-report limitation line), graded on the report file.

## PR-05 [high] The adversarial query is hard-wired to the null direction, so negative and debunking verdicts never meet their strongest counter-evidence

EVIDENCE: tool-catalog.md:49-52 'run one deliberately adversarial query - search for the negative or null result explicitly (`AND (negative OR null OR "failed to")`... IMPACT: The mandatory 'adversarial query on every run' contract is defeated for every negative or 'unsettled' verdict: the only counter-search reinforces the verdict.
