# Psychopharmacology MOC

Umbrella map for drug-class and drug-mechanism work: approval cohorts, pipeline surveillance,
dose and receptor references, and comparative-efficacy landscapes across psychotropic classes.
Disorder-level reviews live under their own disorder MOC; decisions about a single patient-level
question live in their own artifacts and route through `pubmed-research-note`.

Room here for: antidepressants, antipsychotics, mood stabilisers, anxiolytics/hypnotics,
stimulants, and cross-class pharmacokinetic/pharmacogenomic references.
