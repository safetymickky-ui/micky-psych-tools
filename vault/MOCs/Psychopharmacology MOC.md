# Psychopharmacology MOC

Map of content for psychopharmacology — drug-level evidence reports and contested claims,
interaction and safety verdicts, drug-class approval cohorts and pipeline surveillance, and
cross-class dose, receptor, and pharmacogenomic references. Disorder-level reviews live under
their own disorder MOC; single patient-level questions route through `pubmed-research-note`.

Room here for: antidepressants, antipsychotics, mood stabilisers, anxiolytics/hypnotics,
stimulants, and analgesics with psychotropic claims.

## Artifacts

- [[Lithium Serum Level — Target for Efficacy Against Adverse Effects]] — which serum lithium
  concentration to aim for and when to accept a lower one: 0.6–0.8 mmol/L as the adult
  maintenance band (0.6 a floor, not a midpoint), 0.8–1.0 for manic-predominant illness and
  acute mania, 0.4–0.6 for good-response/poor-tolerance and for older adults; ≥0.5 for unipolar
  augmentation. Adjudicates the level–harm literature — toxicity episodes and interacting drugs,
  not the 0.65-vs-0.85 difference, drive renal decline (confidence moderate).
- [[Novel Antidepressants — FDA-Approved and in the Pipeline — Comprehensive Review]] — the
  2009–2025 FDA approval cohort drug-by-drug (esketamine, dextromethorphan–bupropion,
  brexanolone, zuranolone, vortioxetine, vilazodone, levomilnacipran, gepirone ER) plus the
  phase 3 pipeline as a landscape; FDA/EMA divergence, the effect-size controversy against the
  6.5-point MADRS threshold, and the kappa-opioid mechanism failures of 2025–2026.
- [[Tramadol as a Psychotropic — Verdict on an Independent Mood Effect]] — whether tramadol has
  a mood effect of its own beyond analgesia; unsettled, leaning no (confidence moderate).
