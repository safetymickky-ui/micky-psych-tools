# Obesity and Cognition

Topic map for the relationship between adiposity, its treatment, and cognitive function in
adults. Both reviews below share one anchor finding: the decade-long lifestyle-intervention
trial in overweight/obese adults with type 2 diabetes was negative for adjudicated cognitive
impairment (MCI 6.4% vs 6.6%; dementia 1.8% vs 1.8%), and cognition tracked neither weight
change nor fitness change.

## Artifacts

- [[Exercise and Cognitive Function in Adults with Obesity]] — does exercise protect or improve
  cognition here? Verdict: prescribe it, but not for cognition; defensible effect d ~ 0.05-0.13,
  no trial-level dementia prevention. Confidence moderate-low.
- [[Weight Loss and Cognitive Function in Adults with Obesity]] — does losing weight recover
  cognition, by any route (lifestyle, GLP-1, bariatric)? Verdict: no, and the observational
  literature is confounded by prodromal weight loss beginning a decade pre-diagnosis. Every
  GLP-1 randomised primary endpoint is null. Confidence moderate-high for the negative.
