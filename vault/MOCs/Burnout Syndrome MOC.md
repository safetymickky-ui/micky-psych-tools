# Burnout Syndrome

Topic map for burnout as it presents in the clinical workforce. The through-line of
everything filed here is that **the measurement problem is upstream of every other question**:

> 182 studies of physician burnout used at least 142 different definitions of it, and
> prevalence across them ran 0%–80.5%. Any burnout number quoted without its instrument and
> its cutoff is not a finding.

Three secondary through-lines worth carrying out of this topic. **The nosology is unresolved
and the clinical rule is not** — burnout and depression correlate at r ≈ 0.40–0.52, which
settles nothing about constructs and settles everything about practice, since missing a
depressive episode in an exhausted clinician is the asymmetric error. **The effect is where
the trials are not** — organisation-directed interventions outperform physician-directed ones
roughly 2.5-fold (SMD −0.45 vs −0.18), yet the Cochrane evidence base holds 117 individual-level
trials against 7 studies of work-related risk factors with no pooled estimate. And **symptom
response is not functional recovery** — internet-CBT moves perceived stress at d = 1.09 while
leaving work ability untouched, tertiary interventions shorten time-to-return (HR 4.5) without
raising full return (OR 1.33), and ten years after rehabilitation 73% of the still-employed
had changed workplace and 31.5% had cut their hours.
