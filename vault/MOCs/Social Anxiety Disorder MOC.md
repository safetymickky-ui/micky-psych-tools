# Social Anxiety Disorder

Topic map for treating social anxiety disorder psychologically. The manual filed here is
built to be used, not only read: every technique it recommends is traced back to the model
that licenses it and the trial that tested it.

The through-line worth keeping in view across everything filed here:

> Roughly half of even the best treatment's effect is non-specific — and the field's apparent
> disagreements are mostly disagreements about the control condition, not the treatment.

Individual cognitive therapy on the Clark and Wells model has the largest effect in the field's
anchor network meta-analysis (SMD −1.56 vs waitlist), and it is one of only two classes —
alongside the antidepressants — that beats its own matched placebo rather than only waitlist.
That specificity is real, but modest: −0.56 vs psychological placebo, almost exactly the
antidepressants' −0.44 vs pill placebo. Waitlist itself is the biggest confound in the
literature, inflating apparent effects by half a standard deviation to two-thirds of one. And
the disorder remains a hard one to treat: social anxiety disorder has among the lowest
remission rates of any anxiety disorder under CBT.
